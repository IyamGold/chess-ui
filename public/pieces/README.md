# Chess piece artwork

The twelve transparent PNGs were supplied by the project team in `chess pieces.zip` on 2026-11-09. They are copied byte-for-byte, with descriptive filenames; no recolouring, tracing or background removal was applied. The king has a cross and the queen has a pointed crown. Each image is 300×300 and retains its original transparency.

`artwork-manifest.json` records the supplied filenames and SHA-256 hashes. Board pieces, promotion choices and the setup colour selector use this set. The Chess wordmark and loading knight remain the established brand symbol.
